## Milestones

| Date | What changed |
|------|--------------|
| 5 Oct 2021 | First version: an Excel sheet for measuring metric threads over wires, with manual deviations |
| 19 Nov 2026 | The spreadsheet learns the ISO 965-1 tables, with the standard's formulas for the gaps |
| 20 Nov 2026 | Threadwise: the spreadsheet becomes a web app with Measure, Make and Find, 251 threads in eight families, in English and Icelandic, with every number checked against published tables |
