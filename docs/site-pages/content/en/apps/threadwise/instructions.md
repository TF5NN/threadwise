[Open the app](https://tf5nn.github.io/threadwise/). Pick a tab at the top.

## Measure

1. Choose the thread type and size, or type your own diameter and pitch (metric, unified and Whitworth).
2. Under **Wires**, measure the wires you have and type their diameter. The reading changes about three times as much as the wire does, so the wrong wire gives the wrong answer. The bar shows where your wire sits between the smallest and largest that work for that thread form.
3. Choose the tolerance class, or **Manual** for limits from a drawing.
4. Aim for the **target**. To check a part, type the micrometer reading under **Check a measurement**.

## Make

1. Choose the thread, **External** or **Internal**, and the class.
2. **Sizes** gives the diameter to turn or bore, and the pitch-diameter limits.
3. For internal threads, **Tap drill** lists drills near 75 % of thread; green ones fall inside the class's minor-diameter limits.
4. **Single-point threading** gives the depth, the compound angle (half the thread angle less ½°) and a pass schedule. Switch between straight radial infeed and the compound.

## Find

1. Choose **Bolt** or **Nut or hole**.
2. Measure the diameter with a caliper: over the crests on a bolt, across the crests inside a hole (that is the minor diameter, not the size).
3. Add the pitch: from a gauge in mm or TPI, or measure the length over 10 or more threads.
4. If you know the flank angle or whether it is tapered, add that. **Flank angle from two wire sizes** works it out from two over-wire readings.
5. The list is ranked closest first, with warnings for look-alikes. Check the top few with a gauge or a known nut or bolt.
